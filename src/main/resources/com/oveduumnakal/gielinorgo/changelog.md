# 1.0.0 - Unreleased

## Quick Overview

Gielinor Go is a creature-raising side game for RuneLite. Monsters near you turn wild, and you catch them with jars.
Your XP in any skill trains a team of six that learns moves, evolves and hatches from eggs.
Battles play themselves, or you can choose each turn.
You can fight wild creatures, eight City Champions, the Elite challenge and your friends.
A Creature Log, daily tasks and daily events give you goals.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Wild creatures and jars

- **Wild creatures.** Monsters near you turn wild as their own smaller copies. Wild creatures never need a second
  player and do not appear in instances. (#1, #15, #50, #60)
- **Catching.** Right-click a wild creature to see your odds, then throw a jar. A knocked-out creature is twice as
  easy to catch. (#17, #32, #51)
- **Spawn variety.** Featured species, night spawns and weekend events change what turns wild. (#27)

### Your team

- **Training and the lead creature.** Every XP drop trains your team. The lead learns twice as fast and follows you.
  (#3, #29, #30, #57)
- **Unique creatures.** Hidden stat rolls, temperaments and nicknames make every creature different. (#23, #25, #61)
- **Evolutions, eggs, moves and healing items.** Over 150 evolutions, eggs from battle wins, up to four moves per
  creature and healing items for use in and out of battle. (#1, #21, #24, #52, #62)

### Battles

- **Battles that play themselves.** Your team fights in front of you, with floating text over each hit.
  (#14, #19, #31, #55, #56, #59)
- **Styles and status effects.** Melee, Ranged and Magic beat each other, and some moves poison, burn, freeze or
  stun. (#22)
- **Choose your own turns.** An option makes each turn wait for you: Attack, Switch, Catch, Item or Run. (#20)

### Goals

- **Creature Log.** Every species you have caught, seen or still need, with rewards at five milestones. (#26)
- **Daily and weekly tasks.** Three tasks a day and one a week, paid when you finish them. (#28)
- **City Champions and the Elite challenge.** Eight champions, eight sigils and the Grand Champion title.
  (#1, #26, #72)

### Friends

- **Friend battles and trades.** Everyone in your RuneLite party who plays shows up on the Battle tab. Everything
  else in the game works on your own.

### Panel

- **Change log.** This window, with a What's New badge after an update. (#80)

### New in this release

- **Catch when nobody can fight.** With no team or a tired lead, a left click on a wild creature throws a jar.
  (#82)
- **Left-click setting.** Choose whether a left click on a wild creature is yours always, never, or only out of
  combat. (#87)
- **Report a bug and Request a feature.** The Feedback button in the panel opens GitHub with your text filled in.
  (#81)
- **Skilling encounters.** Fishing, Woodcutting, Mining, Farming and Hunter sometimes bring a wild creature
  beside you, and three species are only met this way. (#90)
- **Boss eggs.** Your first kill of a boss always drops its egg, later kills 1 in 20; a raid gives its final
  boss. (#91)
- **Days played.** The login streak became a count of days played that never resets; every 7th day still pays
  an egg. (#89)
- **Sound effects.** Game sounds for a catch, a level-up, an evolution, a hatch, a gilded creature and other
  big moments, with a setting to turn them off. (#94)
- **A reward for releasing.** Releasing creatures pays creature XP to your lead, and Storage can release several
  at once. (#92)
- **Reset progress.** A link at the bottom of the Team tab starts you over after two clicks; the old save is kept
  aside. (#84)
- **Starter creatures.** New players pick an Imp, a Gnome Archer or a Wizard at level 5; the Gnome Archer starts a
  new evolution line. (#83)
- **Richer Log entries.** Click a species in the Creature Log for its examine text, evolution line and how many
  you caught; right-click it to open its wiki page. (#93)
- **Log rewards and titles.** The Creature Log pays at 750 species, 1,000 and a complete Log, which earns the
  Master Collector title; with Grand Champion you pick which one the header shows. (#88)
- **Evolutions by night or underground.** Some creatures evolve only at night or in a cave, and the Rat and Wolf
  lines branch. (#99)
- **Five combat styles.** Melee splits into Stab, Slash and Crush, each with its own jar from Attack, Strength or
  Defence XP. (#85)
- **A rival.** Bryn takes the starter that beats yours and challenges you five times from the Battle tab. (#95)
