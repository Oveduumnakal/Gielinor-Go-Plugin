# 1.0.0 - Unreleased

## Quick Overview

Gielinor Go is a creature-raising side game for RuneLite. Monsters near you turn wild, and you catch them with jars.
Your XP in any skill trains a team of six that learns moves, evolves and hatches from eggs.
Battles play themselves, or you can choose each turn.
You can fight wild creatures, eight City Champions, the Elite challenge and your friends.
A Creature Log, daily tasks and daily events give you goals.

## Detailed Breakdown

### Wild creatures and jars

- **Wild creatures.** Monsters near you turn wild as their own smaller copies. Wild creatures never need a second
  player and do not appear in instances. (#1, #15, #50, #60)
- **Catching.** Right-click a wild creature for Battle and a Catch list of your jars, best odds first, then throw one.
  A knocked-out creature is twice as easy to catch. (#17, #32, #51, #210)
- **Wild creature bubbles.** Move the mouse near a wild creature and three bubbles pop out above it: throw the
  selected jar, start a battle, or read a summary card. A plain left click now just walks. (#264)
- **Spawn variety.** Featured species, night spawns and weekend events change what turns wild. (#27)

### Your team

- **Training and the lead creature.** Every XP drop trains your team. The lead learns twice as fast and follows you.
  (#3, #29, #30, #57)
- **Unique creatures.** Hidden stat rolls, temperaments and nicknames make every creature different. (#23, #25, #61)
- **Evolutions, eggs, moves and healing items.** Over 150 evolutions, eggs from battle wins, up to four moves per
  creature and healing items for use in and out of battle. (#1, #21, #24, #52, #62)

### Battles

- **Battles that play themselves.** Your team fights in front of you, with floating text over each hit.
  (#14, #19, #31, #55, #56, #59)
- **Styles and status effects.** Melee, Ranged and Magic beat each other, and some moves poison, burn, freeze or
  stun. (#22)
- **Choose your own turns.** An option makes each turn wait for you: Attack, Switch, Catch, Item or Run. (#20)

### Goals

- **Creature Log.** Every species you have caught, seen or still need, with rewards at five milestones. (#26)
- **Daily and weekly tasks.** Three tasks a day and one a week, paid when you finish them. (#28)
- **City Champions and the Elite challenge.** Eight champions, eight sigils and the Grand Champion title.
  (#1, #26, #72)
- **Champion card edge.** The colored stripe on a champion card now follows the card's rounded corners. (#279)
- **Sigil badges.** Champion cards and the champions heading now show a shield badge, plain for a sigil and filled
  with a star for a gold sigil. (#280)

### Friends

- **Friend battles and trades.** Everyone in your RuneLite party who plays shows up on the Battle tab. Everything
  else in the game works on your own.

### Panel

- **Change log.** This window, with a What's New badge after an update. (#80)

### New in this release

- **More menu on the Party page.** The "Start over?" box, the footer's Change log and Feedback buttons and the
  key hint line at the bottom of the window are gone, and the page area now fills the window with the same margin
  all round. A More button next to Pin Party opens a menu with Change log, Feedback, Controls (the keys) and
  Start over. Hover Feedback to open its list to the right. Start over still asks first. The Show key hints setting
  is gone. (#289)
- **No sidebar jar.** The jar button in RuneLite's sidebar is gone; the jar on the game screen still opens the
  window. (#267)
- **Search the Collection.** Type part of a name in the search box above the list to find a creature. It works
  with the Show filter, and creatures you have not met stay hidden. (#278)
- **Green checkmark for caught.** A creature you have caught before now shows a green checkmark instead of a
  green jar, on its name tag and its info card. (#276)
- **No rarity bar.** The coloured bar on the edge of creature portraits is gone; the rarity is still named in
  the text. (#275)
- **Party backs out of Summary.** Clicking the Party tab while a creature's Summary is open takes you back to the
  Party list. (#282)
- **Resize the window by dragging.** Drag the grip in the window's bottom-right corner, or its right or bottom edge.
  The window starts Small, and Alt + right-click brings it back to Small. The Size button is gone. (#271)
- **Reset with Alt + right-click.** Hold Alt and right-click the window to put it back to its normal size and place.
  The Reset window button is gone. (#269)
- **One window look.** The window now uses only the RuneLite dark style; the style setting and the footer style
  button are gone. (#270)
- **Header underline fits.** The line under the active tab now ends where the slanted tab ends. (#272)
- **Catch when nobody can fight.** With no team or a tired lead you can still catch: use the jar bubble or Catch
  in the right-click menu. (#82)
- **Report a bug and Request a feature.** Feedback in the More menu opens GitHub with your text filled in.
  (#81)
- **Sharper icons and bubbles.** Text and icons stay crisp at any window size or UI scale. The wild creature
  bubbles became rings with a clear middle, the Battle bubble shows the game's Combat tab icon, and the jars you own
  open as an even honeycomb with easier-to-read counts. (#266)
- **Pin Party.** A button on the Party page, and a setting, show your team
  (portraits, names, levels and health) in a box on the game screen. (#268)
- **A more natural follower.** Your lead keeps a small gap, lags a step or two behind while you walk, then
  catches up, instead of hugging your heels. It still keeps up through teleports and map loads. (#302)
- **One size on the Party view.** The gender and style icons now match the size of the text beside them. (#273)
- **HP and XP side by side.** A Party row shows the health bar and the experience bar next to each other. (#274)
- **Party highlight.** The ring that follows your keyboard on the Party page has its own colour and now sits right
  on the row instead of beside it. (#277)
- **Stab icon.** The dagger now points up and is as big as the other combat style icons. (#283)
- **Gender icon size.** The gender sign is now the same size as the combat style icon beside it. (#285)
- **Blue and pink gender signs.** The male sign is blue and the female sign is pink. (#284)
- **Daily and Weekly apart.** A line now separates the Daily tasks from the Weekly one on the Tasks page. (#288)
- **A better pin.** The pin button on tasks and eggs is now a drawn push pin: tilted and outlined when free, upright
  and filled when pinned. (#287)
- **Clearer Bag bars.** The Trainer level bar and the Next jar bar now use clearly different colours, easy to tell
  apart even for colour-blind players. (#290)
- **Faster jar list.** The jar bubble opens its list after half a second of hovering, twice as fast as before.
  (#301)
- **Two-step close.** When the mouse leaves a wild creature, the jar list folds away first and the three bubbles
  follow. (#300)
- **Steady bubbles.** The bubbles stay put while your mouse is on one, even if the creature walks, and glide back
  to it a second after you move off. (#293)
- **Bubble tooltips.** The jar bubbles show the jar and its catch chance, Battle says Start combat and Info says
  Information. (#292)
- **Skilling encounters.** Fishing, Woodcutting, Mining, Farming and Hunter sometimes bring a wild creature
  beside you, and three species are only met this way. (#90)
- **Boss eggs.** Your first kill of a boss always drops its egg, later kills 1 in 20; a raid gives its final
  boss. (#91)
- **Days played.** The login streak became a count of days played that never resets; every 7th day still pays
  an egg. (#89)
- **Sound effects.** Game sounds for a catch, a level-up, an evolution, a hatch, a gilded creature and other
  big moments, with a setting to turn them off. (#94)
- **A reward for releasing.** Releasing creatures pays creature XP to your lead, and Storage can release several
  at once. (#92)
- **Reset progress.** A link at the bottom of the Team tab starts you over after two clicks; the old save is kept
  aside. (#84)
- **Starter creatures.** New players pick an Imp, a Gnome Archer or a Wizard at level 5; the Gnome Archer starts a
  new evolution line. (#83)
- **Richer Log entries.** Click a species in the Creature Log for its examine text, evolution line and how many
  you caught; right-click it to open its wiki page. (#93)
- **Log rewards and titles.** The Creature Log pays at 750 species, 1,000 and a complete Log, which earns the
  Master Collector title; with Grand Champion you pick which one the header shows. (#88)
- **Evolutions by night or underground.** Some creatures evolve only at night or in a cave, and the Rat and Wolf
  lines branch. (#99)
- **Five combat styles.** Melee splits into Stab, Slash and Crush, each with its own jar from Attack, Strength or
  Defence XP. (#85)
- **Species stats.** Every species has its own health, attack, defence and speed from the real monster, within 25%,
  and evolving never lowers one. (#86)
- **A rival.** Bryn takes the starter that beats yours and challenges you five times from the Battle tab. (#95)
- **Bond.** Your lead earns hearts as it walks and wins with you: a heart on its name tag, and up to 5% more XP.
  (#96)
- **Champion rematches.** After the Elite clear each City Champion fights again with six creatures at levels 80 to 99,
  for a gold sigil and a daily reward. (#97)
- **Choose your turns against friends.** When you both have Choose battle actions on, a friend battle is played
  turn by turn. (#98)
- **Painted window.** A new in-game window drawn on the game screen, with six tabs and two looks. The jar button on
  the game screen opens and closes it. (#223)
- **Party and Summary.** Your team of six as rows with portrait, nickname, gender, style, level and health and XP
  bars; click one for its Summary. (#224)
- **Battle buttons on the game screen.** While a battle waits for your choice, the box on the game screen has
  buttons for Attack, Switch, Catch, Item and Run, so you can steer a battle without hotkeys. (#225)
- **Painted window tabs.** The painted window has Party, Bag, Storage, Collection, Tasks and Battle, plus an in-window
  change log and a Feedback menu. (#226)
- **The side panel is gone.** The jar on the game screen opens the painted window, and there is no hidden
  old-panel setting any more. (#227)
- **Longer walks.** A Bronze jar now takes 300 tiles, eggs take 1,000 to 4,000 tiles and the walking tasks ask for
  twice as many tiles. Eggs you already carry keep their old target. (#206)
- **Rarer finds.** Stronger monsters turn wild less often: Uncommon at 0.6 times the chance, Rare 0.35, Epic 0.15 and
  Legendary 0.05. The Wild creature rarity setting stays the rate for Common ones. (#213)
- **Rarer skilling finds.** Creatures that come while you train a skill now follow the same tier factors, so
  Rare ones come about a third less often at level 99 and the Epic River troll, Rock Golem and Tree spirit about one
  encounter in 133. (#220)
- **Seen and caught marks.** A mark beside each wild creature's name shows whether its species is new to you,
  seen or already caught. (#209)
- **Wild creature tooltip.** Hover a wild creature to see its name, level, style and whether you have seen or caught
  its species. (#205)
- **Hunter creatures.** Pyre foxes, kebbits, salamanders, moths, antelope, implings and other Hunter creatures with
  no combat level now turn wild and can be battled and caught; their level is their Hunter level. (#207)
- **Sort and filter Storage.** Sort stored creatures by level, species, rarity, date caught or nickname, and filter
  them by level, style, rarity, rating, gender, gilded or nickname, showing or hiding each. (#212)
- **Wandering lead.** When you stand still your lead creature wanders around within 3 tiles of you, and walks back
  to your trail when you set off. A setting turns it off. (#208)
- **Pinned tracker.** Pin daily and weekly tasks and carried eggs to a small box on the game screen with live
  progress. (#216)
- **Storage views.** Switch Storage between a list, a Box grid you can rearrange by dragging, and a list grouped
  by species. (#211)
- **XP while you are away.** XP you gain on mobile, in another client or with the plugin off trains your team the next
  time you log in, with jars for the skills and for level-ups. (#219)
- **Details for stored creatures.** Storage creatures have the same Summary as your team, with the real stat numbers
  and the day you caught them. (#215)
- **Fainting.** A creature that reaches 0 health faints and stays down until you revive it in a city or at a bank
  (or with a Revive draught anywhere); it comes back at 50%. (#221)
- **Stronger hatchlings.** Rarer eggs hatch at a higher level: Common 1, Uncommon 5, Rare 10, Epic 20 and Legendary
  30, boss eggs too. (#220)
- **Battle balance.** Wild creatures now appear within one level of their usual level, Hex hits as hard as a Magic
  bolt, and Rare Magic creatures are a little tougher so no Rare style is left behind. Friend battles need the same
  version on both sides. (#220)
- **New style icons.** Ranged, Magic, Stab, Slash and Crush have new pictures wherever a creature or move shows its
  style. (#258)
- **New jar pictures.** All 17 jars have new artwork wherever a jar is shown: the Bag, the throw, and the floating
  lines. (#261)
- **Window closes for a battle.** Starting any battle now closes the Gielinor Go window at once, so the battle is
  in view and no "in battle" message is left on screen. (#296)
- **Battle result at the end.** A battle that plays itself now pays its XP and rewards, and faints a creature, only
  after the last beat has shown, and the window stays shut until then. (#295)
- **One cast per Magic attack.** A Magic attack now shows one cast and one projectile, even when the same beat is
  played again. (#297)
- **Random events.** A random event NPC no longer counts as a fight, so the window stays open. (#291)
- **Reviving.** Fainted creatures now stand up by themselves at 50% when you reach a bank or a city, and a fainted
  creature moved into Storage is revived. (#298)
- **Movesets list.** A new page in the docs lists every creature and every move it can learn. (#299)
- **New Summary page.** Two columns: a header with the style badge, name, gender and level, the chips, a big
  picture and the description with what it grows into (shown as ??? until you have seen it) on the left, and the
  stat bars, nature, bond hearts and move pills on the right. (#281)
- **Glass look.** Windows, boxes, the Info card and wild bubbles are frosted glass that takes its colours from the
  game around them; soft raised buttons and pressed-in bars. The Info card has a thin blue edge until you click it.
  Settings > Glass look turns it off for the solid look. (#324)
- **One shape everywhere.** Every part of the window now has the slanted shape. (#321)

### Fixes

- **Highlight shape.** The highlight on slanted buttons was a gold rectangle; it now follows the button's shape.
  (#322)
- **Start-up.** The plugin starts again, and the client no longer stops near a wild creature. (#152)
- **Saves.** Closing the client, switching profile or turning the plugin off no longer loses progress or writes
  into the wrong save, and a waiting boss egg stays with its own save. (#126, #127, #131, #132)
- **Daily rules.** Setting the clock back no longer resets daily tasks or rematch rewards, the starter offer stays
  the same after a relog, and the first XP drop after turning the plugin on trains your team. (#141, #142, #149)
- **Friend battles.** A chosen-turns battle no longer stops at the start, a late accept no longer ends the battle
  you are in, items are never used up, both sides share one turn clock, and a friend's switch shows before your
  hit. (#123, #124, #136, #143, #144, #145, #148)
- **Trades.** Accept agrees only to the offer you saw, a busy party no longer drops a confirm, a trade that
  confirms mid-battle waits for the battle to end, and a friend can no longer block a reset. (#122, #128, #146,
  #147)
- **Panel and team.** Release and team moves act on the creature you clicked, Storage ticks stay, new creatures go
  to storage during an Elite run, and Battle shows whenever a battle can start. (#125, #129, #130, #139)
- **Changing your team.** You can now move creatures between your team and Storage in Varlamore, Zeah and Prifddinas
  towns and other cities, next to any bank (even one that was already in view), and while the bank is open.
  (#202, #203)
- **Battle scenes.** A map load no longer forfeits a battle, and status tags sit on the right creature and clear
  when the status ends, a reply waits until a melee attacker is back on its spot, defeated creatures lie on top of
  the ground, and a shortened team battle always shows both creatures attacking. (#133, #137, #138, #199, #200,
  #201)
- **Spell projectiles.** Bind, Hex, Flame Lash and Hellfire show their own bolt instead of the Fire Strike
  bolt. (#197)
- **World.** The follower and the arena foe stay on your side of walls, projectile colours no longer change other
  models, floating text stays on its tile, a model loads once, and water creatures such as the dolphin stand on top
  of the ground. (#134, #135, #140, #150, #151, #218)
